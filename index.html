About me

I’m Tanmay, widely known as Silent Programmer, a web developer and programmer based in Uttar Pradesh, India. With a strong background in web development, I specialize in building responsive, user-friendly websites that are both functional and visually appealing. My passion lies in solving complex problems through code, creating seamless digital experiences that prioritize User Experience, accessibility, and innovation.

In addition to my development and design expertise, I’m a Technical Writer and community contributor. I love sharing my knowledge through platforms like my blog and FreeCodeCamp, where I write about Frontend technologies, web security, and programming to inspire and educate fellow developers. I’ve also created educational tools like ‘tphisher’ for phishing simulation and a car racing game in HTML, all designed to help others learn and grow. Whether I’m coding a responsive website, designing an engaging UI, or contributing to the developer community, my goal is to create solutions that are innovative, impactful, and accessible to all users, including the visually impaired.
